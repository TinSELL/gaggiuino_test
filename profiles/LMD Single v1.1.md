专为单份 7g 阶梯式粉碗 (stepdown basket) 中的中度至中深烘焙咖啡豆设计，目标是在尽量减少深烘焙豆常见的过度萃取的同时，优化萃取的均匀度。这是通过在萃取过程中将压力目标过渡到流量目标来实现的。由于粉饼体积小，流量极其受限。

[SproFiler.io 萃取示例](https://sprofiler.io/shot/9fe30b23-b787-437f-85f2-cbce53dd1070)

**冲煮准备：**
- VST 或 LM "7g" 单份粉碗（不是高萃取 HE 粉碗）
- 推荐使用 8g 粉（大约 7-9g 应该都可以工作；根据需要调整基于重量停止的数值）

**主要目标：**
- 预浸泡 (PI)：由流量驱动，直至达到 1.2 bar
- 由于粉饼体积小，没有浸泡 (Soak) 阶段
- 萃取：爬升到 7 bar，在 3 秒内降低 1 bar，然后随着粉饼研磨度允许的情况过渡到由流量定义的曲线。
- 重量：16 克（基于重量停止），1:2 的粉液比

**注意事项：**
- 预设了一些“挽救萃取”的过渡参数（例如，预浸泡期间杯中如果出现一克液体，会将配置文件推进到萃取阶段）。如果跳过了某个阶段或时间较短，可能是触发了其中一个条件。
- 如果在萃取接近尾声时压力逐渐减弱，这是正常的。

*Original text:*
Designed for medium to medium-dark beans in a single-shot 7 g stepdown basket, the goal is to optimize for even extraction while minimizing the overextraction that can be common with darker beans. This is done by transitioning from a pressure target to a flow target during extraction. Flow is extremely limited due to the small puck volume

[SproFiler.io example shot](https://sprofiler.io/shot/9fe30b23-b787-437f-85f2-cbce53dd1070)

**Shot Prep:**  
- VST or LM "7g" single basket (not HE)
- 8 g recommended (should work from 7-9 g; adjust stop-on-weight as needed)

**Main targets:**  
- PI: Flow driven until 1.2 bar
- No soak due to small puck volume
- Extraction: Climb to 7 bar, decrease 1 bar over 3 sec, then transition to flow defined profile as the puck grind allows.
- Weight: 16 g stop-on-weight, 1:2 ratio 

**Notes:**  
- There are some "save the shot" transition parameters defined (e.g. a gram in the cup during PI will move the profile on to extraction). If a phase is skipped or short one of those was likely triggered.
- It's fine if pressure tapers off toward the end of the shot.
